# Dhaka Gadget Grove

# Project: Build a High-Converting Bangladeshi Tech E-Commerce Website

## 1. Your Role

Act as a senior full-stack developer, e-commerce UX/UI designer, conversion rate optimization (CRO) specialist, and digital commerce strategist.

Your task is to design and develop a **modern, dynamic, premium-quality, fully responsive tech e-commerce website for the Bangladeshi market**.

The website should not look like a generic online store template. It should feel like a trusted, established Bangladeshi technology retailer with a polished user experience, compelling product presentation, and a seamless shopping journey.

The primary business goal is to **increase sales, improve conversion rates, increase average order value, encourage repeat purchases, and build long-term customer loyalty**.

All customer-facing website content must be written in **natural, professional Bangla (বাংলা)**. Use Bangladeshi Taka (৳) for prices and local conventions for phone numbers, addresses, delivery information, and customer communication.

Use English only where appropriate for technology brands, model names, technical specifications, and commonly recognized technical terminology.

- `#024950`: Navigation, secondary backgrounds, product information sections, and dark interactive elements.
- `#964734`: Carefully selected promotional badges, special offers, discount indicators, and important secondary calls to action.
- `#0FA4AF`: Primary action buttons, links, active states, interactive elements, and highlights.
- `#AFDDE5`: Light backgrounds, category panels, subtle highlights, and supporting visual elements.

Use white and neutral gray where necessary to maintain readability and visual balance.

Maintain strong text contrast, consistent spacing, accessible button states, and a coherent design system.

Avoid excessive gradients, unnecessary glassmorphism, cluttered layouts, and oversized decorative elements.

The overall visual direction should be modern, trustworthy, technology-focused, clean, and commercially effective.

## 3. Target Audience

Design the website primarily for customers in Bangladesh, including:

- University students purchasing laptops, headphones, and accessories.
- Freelancers and remote workers upgrading their workstations.
- Gamers looking for gaming peripherals and performance equipment.
- Office professionals purchasing productivity devices.
- Technology enthusiasts looking for the latest gadgets.
- Families shopping for everyday electronic products.
- Budget-conscious customers comparing prices and specifications.

The interface should be intuitive for both experienced online shoppers and first-time e-commerce customers.

Prioritize mobile users because many Bangladeshi customers shop using smartphones.
